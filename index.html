A ROGUELIKE WHERE YOUR SHIP IS THE RUN

Fast 2D top-down combat with inertia-based movement. Choose a ship, enter generated encounters, clear pirate waves, and decide which strange new module is worth rebuilding around.

01

FOUR TRIGGERS, FOUR DECISIONS

Equip up to four independently fired weapons. Range, timing, target choice, and energy use all matter — firing everything at once is how builds die.

02

MODULAR EQUIPMENT

Shape every weapon with branching upgrade paths — armor, reactors, amplifiers, vector drives, and risk-reward modules that change how the whole ship flies.

03

LET THE RUN CHANGE YOUR PLAN

Weighted loot rewards, six rarity tiers, and pity-driven enemy drops. A strong item can redirect the run; a greedy choice can end it.

04

ESCALATING ENCOUNTERS

Pirate fighters, snipers, destroyers, asteroid fields, elites, and pirate-base bosses each pressure a different weakness in your loadout.

MEET THE ACES

CAM 01 // LIVE

DUTCH

Flies the F-222

Twenty years in the border squadrons and the only thing Dutch trusts is the F-222 he refuses to retire. Doesn't say much; his gun camera does the talking. The younger pilots think he's a legend. Dutch thinks legends get people killed.

CAM 02 // LIVE

ZIGGY

Flies the Tracer

Belt courier turned interceptor ace. Ziggy ran contraband through the Kessler shoals for six years without a scratch; the Tracer's blink drive is wired to her reflexes, not the other way around. Talks fast, flies faster, never finishes a debrief.

CAM 03 // LIVE

MANTIS

Flies the Die Fighter

Nobody knows where Mantis learned to fly, only that the Die Fighter's insectoid silhouette empties a sector's comm channels when it appears. Patient to the point of unnerving; waits in the dark, strikes once, and is gone before the wreckage cools.
